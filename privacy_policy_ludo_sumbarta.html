Ludo Sumbarta

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Applies to the Ludo Sumbarta Android app (package com.sumbarta.ludo)

In short: Ludo Sumbarta does not create accounts and does not run its own servers. Game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and ad-interaction data as described below. Those ad requests are marked child-directed, so they are not used for interest-based personalization.

Information we collect

We do not ask for an account, email address, or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The app does not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. It does use the internet to load advertisements, and Google AdMob may collect the information listed under Advertising.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • game progress, such as stages, cities, keys and items you have unlocked;
  • settings, such as sound on or off;
  • the traveller name and avatar you choose in the game.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup feature, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings and Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Links and sharing

The app has buttons that open web pages, such as this policy or the game's Google Play page, in your browser, and a Share button that lets you send a link to the game using an app you choose. These actions happen only when you tap them, and the pages and apps you open are covered by their own privacy policies.

Advertising and third-party services

We use Google AdMob to show advertisements. Banners appear on menu screens. A rewarded ad plays only if you tap to watch it, and then the game adds coins. Ads are not shown during board play.

Ad requests are configured for a mixed audience of children and young adults: child-directed treatment, under the age of consent, and a maximum content rating of G. The ads are not personalized. We do not use them to build an interest profile of you.

To deliver, measure, and protect those ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID (AAID), when the device still has one;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as ad views and clicks;
  • diagnostic and performance data related to the ad request.

Google uses this data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and keep the service secure. How Google handles it is described in Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can limit ad personalization, or remove the advertising ID, in Android settings:

  • Open Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Delete advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization if that control is shown on your device.

Children

Ludo Sumbarta is for a mixed audience, including children. Ad requests are marked child-directed and are limited to G-rated content. We do not knowingly ask children for an account or for contact details. A parent or guardian can delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes the progress stored on the device.

Where you get the app

If you download the app from Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, itch.io or GitHub, that store or site may collect information about your download under its own privacy policy. This website uses no cookies or tracking scripts; its home page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of delivering them.

Website comments

The comments section on our website uses utterances. To comment you sign in with your GitHub account, and your comment, GitHub username and profile picture are stored publicly as an issue in our GitHub repository, under GitHub's privacy statement. Reading comments does not require an account. You can edit or delete your comments on GitHub at any time. The game app itself has no comments feature.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting information in a new way, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/nisharani1729/ludosumbarta/issues.
