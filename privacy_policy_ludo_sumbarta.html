Ludo Sumbarta

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Applies to the Ludo Sumbarta Android app (package com.sumbarta.ludo)

In short: Ludo Sumbarta does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline, and everything it saves stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app:

  • does not ask for any Android permissions, including internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage;
  • does not create accounts or ask for an email address or phone number;
  • does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software;
  • does not read device identifiers such as the Android ID or advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • game progress, such as stages, cities, keys and items you have unlocked;
  • settings, such as sound on or off;
  • the traveller name and avatar you choose in the game.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup feature, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings and Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Links and sharing

The app has buttons that open web pages, such as this policy or the game's Google Play page, in your browser, and a Share button that lets you send a link to the game using an app you choose. These actions happen only when you tap them, and the pages and apps you open are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

Ludo Sumbarta is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age group.

Where you get the app

If you download the app from Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, itch.io or GitHub, that store or site may collect information about your download under its own privacy policy. This website uses no cookies or tracking scripts; its home page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of delivering them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game starts collecting any information, we will update this page before releasing that version and change the date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/nisharani1729/ludosumbarta/issues.
