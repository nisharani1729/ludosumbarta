### Contributing to the Lore of Ludo Sumbarta

Welcome to the official lore repository for Ludo Sumbarta! We are incredibly excited to build the world of Sumbarta Fort alongside our community. Whether you are fixing a typo, expanding a character's backstory, or translating a story, your contributions help shape this universe. 

By contributing to this repository, you help us build a richer world for players on the Google Play Store. Please review the guidelines below before submitting a Pull Request. 

### Core Rules for Submissions

To keep the universe consistent, all submissions must respect the established game world: 

1. The Six Characters: Do not alter the fundamental traits, identities, or gameplay concepts of the core 6 characters. Your stories should expand on who they are, not rewrite them.
2. Sumbarta Fort: The fort is the central anchor of our world. Stories should connect back to the fort, its history, its defense, or its strategic importance.
3. Tone and Content: Keep stories engaging, strategic, and accessible. Explicit, excessively violent, or offensive content will be immediately rejected.
4. No Code: This is a strict text-and-story repository. Do not upload scripts, game assets, or code frameworks here.

### How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository to your own GitHub account.
2. Create a new branch for your changes (e.g., feature/character-backstory).
3. Make your edits or add your new story file (.md format preferred) inside the appropriate folder.
4. Submit a Pull Request (PR) describing what you added or changed.
