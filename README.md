# Ludo Sumbarta

In the year the meadows still had no kingdom, six people walked out of a reed town and called the grass a road.

They carried one wooden die, one River Oath, and a sentence older than any fort: win the Royal Game at every gate, or the ten clans stay ten names arguing over water—and something older than argument, the King of Serpents, keeps peeling the world back to wilderness.

That walk founded **Sumbarta**.

---

## The kingdom

**Ludo Sumbarta** is an Android board-game adventure set in a kingdom where games are the greatest sport. Fort Sumbarta is the front door: a fortress whose halls and stalls are the game menu. From the courtyard you take Ancient and Future roads, sit a Ludo table, and play the games in the halls.

The crown campaign is the River Oath itself—winding roads, royal keys, and four-player Ludo in every city—until the Crystal Crown at Sphatika.

**Site:** [ludosumbarta.in](https://ludosumbarta.in)

## The party of six

You play as the lead traveler. The road counts six:

| Traveler | Role |
|---|---|
| **Baby Dufaan** | Young adventurer — keeps the wooden die |
| **Kael Gobu** | Calm strategist — writes what the century becomes |
| **Mani Dufaan** | Fearless explorer — never waits for a number |
| **Taro Domizon** | Cheerful inventor — tools before weapons |
| **Maya Mersace** | Clever rogue — knows which roots will hold |
| **Borin Gubatti** | Gentle giant — six straps, six lives |

They are not heroes of a palace. They are weather the land negotiates with.

---

## Takshak's grudge

**Takshak**, the King of Serpents, is not one of the six. From the scorched reed lands south of Sumbarta he keeps an ancient grudge: the kingdom must fall. He never sits at the Ludo table. When a city match begins he may bend a rival, and that rival may cheat. The party walks the road. Takshak works against it.

## Two roads, one kingdom

The same ten cities, twice:

- **Ancient Sumbarta** (1000 BC–100 BC) — River Oath, royal roads, the Great Canal, rival crowns, the Black Monsoon, and the greatest Royal Games.
- **Future Sumbarta** (3000 AD–4000 AD) — the Oath recoded as light: holo-roads, neon city-twins, plasma guilds, shield-arcologies, until Horizon 4000.

Each road ends at a city gate, then a full Ludo board against named rivals.

## How the journey works

1. **Road stages** — keys, mining, shields, tunnels, snakes, pits, monsters, and coins — then the city gate.
2. **City match** — classic four-player Ludo against three rivals, each with a distinct play style.
3. **Objectives** — recover the royal key; on many stages, mine three resource deposits before the gate opens.
4. **Traveler's Home** — grow your homestead in Fort Sumbarta as you conquer cities and earn stars.

In Fort Sumbarta’s courtyard, halls around the Royal Keep also hold **Carrom**, **Callbreak**, **Snakes & Ladders**, **Humming Bird**, the **15-Puzzle**, and more.
