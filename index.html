Ludo Sumbarta

Welcome to Sumbarta, a kingdom where games are the greatest sport, and every hero dreams of becoming the Royal Champion.

Walk into Fort Sumbarta — a fortress whose halls and stalls are the game menu. The crown campaign is Ludo Sumbarta: travel winding roads, recover the royal key, and win four-player Ludo in each of the ten cities.

200 stages across two paths — Ancient Sumbarta (1000 BC–100 BC) and Future Sumbarta (3000 AD–4000 AD) — each revisiting the same ten cities as they rise, prosper, and transform.

Two roads, one kingdom

Swipe between Ancient and Future on the fort map — ten centuries per track, ten cities per century.

Ancient Sumbarta

From the River Oath and the founding of Sumbarta through royal roads, the Great Canal, rival crowns, the Black Monsoon, and the greatest Royal Games beneath uneasy skies — until the six travelers save the kingdom into everlasting prosperity.

Future Sumbarta

In 3000 AD the River Oath is recoded as light, and Sumbarta wakes on holo-roads. Neon twins of every ancient city, plasma guilds, shield-arcologies, and at Horizon 4000 the path becomes a beam of time.

How the journey works

Born of Surya's light — roll the die, walk the road, win the Royal Games.

  1. Road stages — 50 steps of keys, mining, shields, tunnels, snakes, pits, monsters, and coins, then the city gate.
  2. City match — move all four pawns home on a classic Ludo board against three rivals, each with a distinct play style.
  3. Objectives — recover the royal key; on many stages, secure three resource deposits before the gate opens.
  4. Traveler's Home — grow your homestead in Fort Sumbarta as you conquer cities and earn stars.
