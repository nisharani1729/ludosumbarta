Two roads, one kingdom

Ancient Sumbarta (1000 BC–100 BC) and Future Sumbarta (3000 AD–4000 AD) — ten centuries per track, the same ten cities reborn.

1000 BC – 100 BC

Ancient Sumbarta

River Oath, royal roads, the Great Canal, rival crowns, the Black Monsoon, and the greatest Royal Games — until the six travelers save the kingdom into everlasting prosperity.

3000 AD – 4000 AD

Future Sumbarta

The River Oath recoded as light. Holo-roads, neon city-twins, plasma guilds, shield-arcologies — until Horizon 4000, when the path becomes a beam of time.

How the journey works

Born of Surya's light — roll the die, walk the road, win the Royal Games.

  1. 1
    Road stages

    50 steps — keys, mining, shields, tunnels, snakes, pits, monsters, and coins — then the city gate.

  2. 2
    City match

    Classic four-player Ludo against three rivals, each with a distinct play style.

  3. 3
    Objectives

    Recover the royal key; on many stages, mine three resource deposits before the gate opens.

  4. 4
    Traveler's Home

    Grow your homestead in Fort Sumbarta as you conquer cities and earn stars.
